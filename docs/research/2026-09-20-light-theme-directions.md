# Reins light-theme directions

## Research basis

- Linear’s current home page presents agents alongside real work artifacts, with product UI carrying the product story rather than decorative imagery. <https://linear.app/>
- Vercel’s current agentic-infrastructure page groups the offer around explicit capabilities such as orchestration, sandboxes, observability, and workflows. <https://vercel.com/>
- Ramp positions AI as finance-workflow automation and uses product-specific finance language rather than a generic assistant framing. <https://ramp.com/>
- Figma’s current site leads with a collaborative canvas and structured product surfaces. <https://www.figma.com/>

## Findings

The useful current pattern for Reins is not a vague "AI" aesthetic. It is a clear product proof surface: named agents, an authoritative policy gate, a human escalation state, and an evidence timeline in the hero. Light themes should use semantic color only for decisions and active paths, preserve generous whitespace around dense operational data, and avoid decorative gradients, glass panels, or invented social proof.

## Directions to test

1. Editorial Ledger: porcelain canvas, near-black type, cobalt as an active-path accent. Best for credibility and readability.
2. Cloud Control: pale blue-gray canvas, deep slate panels, electric blue as a narrow technical accent. Best for an enterprise-control-plane impression.
3. Mineral Evidence: warm white canvas, graphite UI, restrained terracotta only for pending human review. Best for a human-centered finance narrative.
4. Signal Canvas: white canvas, sharp black typography, lime used only for live/approved state. Best for a memorable developer-and-finance hybrid.

## Recommendation

Editorial Ledger is the leading candidate for Reins. It creates the clearest reading environment for evidence and policy while still allowing a visibly modern product interface. The other three directions are exploration options, not a commitment to production implementation.
